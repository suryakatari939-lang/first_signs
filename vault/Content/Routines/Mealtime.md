# Mealtime

- Routine id: `meal` · When: Breakfast, lunch, dinner · **8 signs**
- Opens in the app at `#routines.meal` (see [[Routine Detail Screen]])

## Signs
- [[EAT]] — Tap the fingertips to your mouth twice.
- [[DRINK]] — Tip the 'cup' up toward your mouth.
- [[MILK]] — Squeeze and open the fist two or three times.
- [[WATER]] — Tap the chin twice.
- [[MORE]] — Tap the fingertips of both hands together twice.
- [[ALL DONE]] — Twist both wrists out so palms face away. Shake once or twice.
- [[HUNGRY]] — Slide down once to your stomach.
- [[WANT]] — Pull toward your body while curling the fingers into claws.

## "Put them together" phrases (`PHRASES.meal`)
- **WANT MORE MILK?** — Do you want more milk?
- **EAT FINISH?** — Are you all done eating?
- **HUNGRY? WANT EAT?** — Are you hungry? Want to eat?
