# Safety & getting out

- Routine id: `safe` · When: On the go · **7 signs**
- Opens in the app at `#routines.safe` (see [[Routine Detail Screen]])

## Signs
- [[STOP]] — Chop the edge down onto the flat palm once, sharply.
- [[HOT]] — Twist quickly away and down.
- [[CAREFUL]] — Tap the top hand on the bottom one twice.
- [[WAIT]] — Wiggle the fingers.
- [[COME]] — Curl them back toward you, like beckoning.
- [[POTTY]] — Shake side to side.
- [[SHOES]] — Knock the thumb sides together twice.

## "Put them together" phrases (`PHRASES.safe`)
- **STOP! WAIT.** — Stop! Wait.
- **CAREFUL, HOT.** — Careful, that's hot.
- **SHOES, COME.** — Shoes on, come on.
