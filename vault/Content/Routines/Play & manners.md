# Play & manners

- Routine id: `play` · When: Daytime · **9 signs**
- Opens in the app at `#routines.play` (see [[Routine Detail Screen]])

## Signs
- [[PLAY]] — Shake both hands by twisting the wrists.
- [[BALL]] — Tap the fingertips together twice.
- [[DOG]] — Pat your thigh, then snap.
- [[HELP]] — Lift both hands up together.
- [[PLEASE]] — Rub in a circle.
- [[THANK YOU]] — Move the hand forward and down toward the person.
- [[SORRY]] — Rub in a circle.
- [[YES]] — Bend the wrist up and down, like a head nodding.
- [[NO]] — Snap the two fingers down onto the thumb.

## "Put them together" phrases (`PHRASES.play`)
- **WANT PLAY BALL?** — Want to play ball?
- **HELP PLEASE.** — Help, please.
- **THANK YOU!** — Thank you!
