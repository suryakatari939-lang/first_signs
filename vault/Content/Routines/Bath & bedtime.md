# Bath & bedtime

- Routine id: `bed` · When: Evening · **6 signs**
- Opens in the app at `#routines.bed` (see [[Routine Detail Screen]])

## Signs
- [[BATH]] — Rub up and down on your chest.
- [[BRUSH TEETH]] — Move side to side.
- [[BOOK]] — Open them like the covers of a book.
- [[BED]] — Rest your cheek on your palm.
- [[SLEEP]] — Draw down over your face as the fingers close. Let your eyes close too.
- [[I LOVE YOU]] — Hold it still, or give it a little shake.

## "Put them together" phrases (`PHRASES.bed`)
- **BATH, BRUSH TEETH, BOOK, BED.** — Bath, teeth, story, bed. Sign the plan before you start.
- **SLEEP. I LOVE YOU.** — Goodnight, I love you.
