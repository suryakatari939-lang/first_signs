# Feelings

- Routine id: `feel` · When: Any time · **7 signs**
- Opens in the app at `#routines.feel` (see [[Routine Detail Screen]])

## Signs
- [[HAPPY]] — Brush upward in small circles, twice.
- [[SAD]] — Move both hands down slowly. Let your face look sad.
- [[MAD]] — Pull out a little while tensing the fingers. Frown.
- [[SCARED]] — Burst both hands open toward each other, as if startled.
- [[HURT]] — Jab the fingertips toward each other twice, or twist them.
- [[TIRED]] — Roll the hands down so they droop. Let your shoulders sag.
- [[LOVE]] — Hug them to your chest.

## "Put them together" phrases (`PHRASES.feel`)
- **YOU SAD? HURT?** — Are you sad? Are you hurt?
- **YOU TIRED. SLEEP SOON.** — You're tired. Sleep soon.
- **MOM LOVE YOU.** — Mommy loves you.
