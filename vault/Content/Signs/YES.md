# YES

Routine: [[Play & manners]] · app id `yes` · defined in `js/signs.js` (`SIGNS` + `CHECKS`)

## How to sign it (shown to the parent)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Hand** | S-hand (fist). |
| **Where** | In front of you. |
| **Move** | Bend the wrist up and down, like a head nodding. |

**Remember it (hook):** Your fist is a nodding head.

**Use it for real (`say`):** Nod your head while you sign it.
