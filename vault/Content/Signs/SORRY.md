# SORRY

Routine: [[Play & manners]] · app id `sorry` · defined in `js/signs.js` (`SIGNS` + `CHECKS`)

## How to sign it (shown to the parent)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Hand** | A-hand (fist, thumb on the side). |
| **Where** | On your chest. |
| **Move** | Rub in a circle. |

**Remember it (hook):** Like PLEASE, but with a fist.

**Use it for real (`say`):** When you make a mistake, sign SORRY to them too.
