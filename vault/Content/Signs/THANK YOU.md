# THANK YOU

Routine: [[Play & manners]] · app id `thanks` · defined in `js/signs.js` (`SIGNS` + `CHECKS`)

## How to sign it (shown to the parent)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Hand** | Flat hand, fingertips at your chin. |
| **Where** | Chin. |
| **Move** | Move the hand forward and down toward the person. |

**Remember it (hook):** Blowing a kiss of thanks.

**Use it for real (`say`):** Sign it to them often. They copy what they see.
