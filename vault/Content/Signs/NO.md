# NO

Routine: [[Play & manners]] · app id `no` · defined in `js/signs.js` (`SIGNS` + `CHECKS`)

## How to sign it (shown to the parent)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Hand** | Index and middle fingers out, thumb open. |
| **Where** | In front of you. |
| **Move** | Snap the two fingers down onto the thumb. |

**Remember it (hook):** A mouth snapping shut on 'no'.

**Use it for real (`say`):** Shake your head with it.

## What the camera coach checks
`CHECKS.no` = `{ hands:1, shape:["two","flatO","index"], at:"body", ref:"tips", move:["squeeze","repeat","close"] }`

- **Hands needed:** 1
- **Accepted handshapes:** two fingers, flat-O, pointing finger → see [[Handshape Detection]]
- **Location:** in front of the body (tracked point = `tips`) → see [[Zones and Body Landmarks]]
- **Movement (any one passes):** `squeeze` (open and close the hand a few times); `repeat` (do the movement twice (tap, tap)); `close` (curl the fingers in) → see [[Movement Detection]]
