# DOG

Routine: [[Play & manners]] · app id `dog` · defined in `js/signs.js` (`SIGNS` + `CHECKS`)

## How to sign it (shown to the parent)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Hand** | Flat hand, then a finger snap. |
| **Where** | At your thigh. |
| **Move** | Pat your thigh, then snap. |

**Remember it (hook):** Calling a dog to come.

**Use it for real (`say`):** Picture books are full of dogs. Point, then sign.
