# DAD

Routine: [[Family]] · app id `dad` · defined in `js/signs.js` (`SIGNS` + `CHECKS`)

## How to sign it (shown to the parent)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Hand** | Open 5-hand. |
| **Where** | Thumb on your forehead. |
| **Move** | Tap the forehead twice. |

**Remember it (hook):** Male signs are often near the forehead.

**Use it for real (`say`):** "DAD home!" when the door opens.
