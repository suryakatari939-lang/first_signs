# Phrases ("Put them together")

`PHRASES` in `js/signs.js`, keyed by routine id; shown under the sign list on each routine page ([[Routine Detail Screen]]). Each is `[ASL gloss, English]`. ASL glosses are written in capitals; yes/no questions use raised eyebrows.

## [[Mealtime]]
- **WANT MORE MILK?** — Do you want more milk?
- **EAT FINISH?** — Are you all done eating?
- **HUNGRY? WANT EAT?** — Are you hungry? Want to eat?

## [[Bath & bedtime]]
- **BATH, BRUSH TEETH, BOOK, BED.** — Bath, teeth, story, bed. Sign the plan before you start.
- **SLEEP. I LOVE YOU.** — Goodnight, I love you.

## [[Feelings]]
- **YOU SAD? HURT?** — Are you sad? Are you hurt?
- **YOU TIRED. SLEEP SOON.** — You're tired. Sleep soon.
- **MOM LOVE YOU.** — Mommy loves you.

## [[Family]]
- **DAD HOME!** — Dad's home!
- **GRANDMA COME?** — Is Grandma coming?

## [[Play & manners]]
- **WANT PLAY BALL?** — Want to play ball?
- **HELP PLEASE.** — Help, please.
- **THANK YOU!** — Thank you!

## [[Safety & getting out]]
- **STOP! WAIT.** — Stop! Wait.
- **CAREFUL, HOT.** — Careful, that's hot.
- **SHOES, COME.** — Shoes on, come on.
