# Alphabet and Numbers (content)

Defined in `js/alphabet.js` (table generated from that file; the source wins). UI in [[ABC Screens]]. Evidence for each description: [[Alphabet Sources and Verification]]. Separate from `SIGNS`/`CHECKS` ([[Routines and Signs Overview]]); **no camera check** (letters like A/S/T/N/M/E are all fists, see [[Handshape Detection]]).

## Data
- `ABC_SOURCES` — 11 reference pages (shown in the app's "Where these descriptions come from" panel on the letters, numbers and spell pages).
- `LETTERS` — 26 items `{id, ch, g, q, src, hand, move?, hook}`; ids `a`..`z`. `g` = group (grouping + quiz look-alike distractors), `q` = search word for the Signing Savvy link, `src` = source ids backing the description.
- `NUMBERS` — 10 items, ids `n1`..`n10`, `g:"num"`.
- `ABC_GROUPS` (6), `NUMBER_TIP`, `SPELL_WORDS`, `SPELL_TIPS` (5).
- Palm rule: faces the viewer except G and H (sideways) and P and Q (pointing down); numbers 1-5 palm toward the signer when counting alone, 6-10 forward. Left-handed signers mirror.

## All letters
| Letter | Group | Hand | Movement | Hook | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Closed fists | Make a fist with your thumb resting against the side of your index finger, pointing up. |  | The thumb stands up beside the fist, like the point of a capital A. | HK, W |
| B | Fingers up | Hold your four fingers straight up and together, with your thumb folded across your palm. |  | A flat board with the thumb tucked in. | HK |
| C | Round shapes | Curve your fingers and thumb into the shape of a C. Face it toward the person you are signing to, not turned sideways. |  | Your hand is the letter. | HK, WB, SSM, SA |
| D | Pointing finger | Point your index finger up. Touch your thumb tip to the tip of your middle finger, with your other fingers curved in toward the thumb. |  | The straight finger is the stem of a d and the circle is its belly. | HK, HS |
| E | Closed fists | Bend your four fingers down so the fingertips rest on top of your thumb, which lies across underneath. Keep it closed, not open. |  | The fingers curl down like a little roof over the thumb. | HK, SSM, WB |
| F | Fingers up | Touch the tip of your thumb to the tip of your index finger to make a small circle. Hold your other three fingers straight up (apart or together). |  | It is the "OK" sign. | HK, SA |
| G | Pointing finger | Turn your hand sideways, palm toward your body. Extend your index finger straight out with your thumb parallel to it. Curl your other fingers into your palm. |  | Show how small something is, sideways. | HK, W, WB, SSM |
| H | Two-finger letters | Turn your hand sideways, palm toward your body. Extend your index and middle fingers together. Keep your other fingers and thumb in a fist. |  | Two fingers lying down like the bar across an H. | HK, W, WB, SSM |
| I | Little finger and thumb | Make a fist, with your thumb closed against your index finger, and stick your little finger straight up. |  | The little finger is the dot of an i. | HK |
| J | Little finger and thumb | Make the I handshape (little finger up). | Twist your wrist so your little finger draws the curve of a J in the air: down, then curve into a hook. | I plus a hook. | W, SA |
| K | Two-finger letters | Point your index finger up and angle your middle finger out from it. Rest your thumb against the side of the middle finger. Palm faces forward. |  | A V with the thumb propped in the middle. | HK, W, SSM |
| L | Pointing finger | Point your index finger up and stick your thumb straight out to the side. Curl your other fingers into your palm. |  | Your thumb and finger draw an L. | HK |
| M | Closed fists | Make a fist with your thumb under your first three fingers (index, middle and ring), so the thumb tip shows between your ring and little fingers. |  | M has three legs, so three fingers go over the thumb. | HK, SA, SSM |
| N | Closed fists | Make a fist with your thumb under your first two fingers (index and middle), so the thumb tip shows between your middle and ring fingers. |  | N has two legs, so two fingers go over the thumb. | HK, SA, SSM |
| O | Round shapes | Curve your fingers and thumb so the tips meet in a round O. Face it toward the person you are signing to and keep it rounded, not flat. |  | Your fingers make the shape of an O. | HK, SSM, WB |
| P | Two-finger letters | Make the K handshape, then drop your wrist so your middle finger points toward the floor. |  | K tipped over to point downward. | W, SA, SSM |
| Q | Pointing finger | Make the G handshape (index finger and thumb straight and parallel), then point them toward the floor. |  | G pointing down, like the tail of a Q. | W, SA |
| R | Two-finger letters | Cross your index and middle fingers and hold them up. Keep your other fingers and thumb in a fist. |  | Fingers crossed, as if wishing for luck. | HK |
| S | Closed fists | Make a fist with your thumb across the front of your index and middle fingers. |  | The thumb crosses in front like a seat belt. | HK, W |
| T | Closed fists | Make a fist with your thumb tucked under your index finger, so the thumb tip shows between your index and middle fingers. |  | T for tucked. | HK, SA, HS |
| U | Two-finger letters | Hold your index and middle fingers straight up and together. Your thumb holds down the other two fingers. |  | Two fingers standing together. | W |
| V | Two-finger letters | Hold your index and middle fingers up and apart. Keep your other fingers and thumb in a fist. |  | U with the fingers apart. The peace sign. | HK |
| W | Fingers up | Hold your index, middle and ring fingers up and apart. Touch your thumb to your little finger. |  | Three fingers make a W. | SSN |
| X | Pointing finger | Make a fist and bend your index finger into a hook. |  | A hooked finger, like a pirate's hook. | HK |
| Y | Little finger and thumb | Stick out your thumb and little finger wide. Curl the other three fingers against your palm. |  | The thumb and little finger are the two arms of a Y. | HK |
| Z | Pointing finger | Point your index finger (a 1 handshape). Use the index finger, not the little finger. | Draw a Z in the air as you would write it: across the top, diagonally down, then across the bottom. | Trace the letter with your finger. | W, SA, SSM, HS |

## Numbers
| Number | Hand | Movement | Hook | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Point your index finger up and fold the other fingers into your palm, with your thumb closed over the middle finger. Palm toward you when counting. |  | One finger for one. | HK, LP, BG |
| 2 | Hold your index and middle fingers up and apart (the V handshape). Hold the other fingers down with your thumb. Palm toward you when counting. |  | Two fingers up, like a peace sign. | LP, BG |
| 3 | Stick out your thumb, index finger and middle finger, spread apart. Fold your other two fingers into your palm. Palm toward you when counting. |  | In ASL the thumb counts: thumb, pointer, middle. | HK, SSN, BG |
| 4 | Hold four fingers up and spread. Fold your thumb against your palm. Palm toward you when counting. |  | Four fingers, thumb tucked away. | HK, BG |
| 5 | Spread all five fingers open, with the thumb out to the side. Palm toward you when counting. |  | A full open hand. | HK, BG |
| 6 | Touch your thumb to your little finger. Hold your index, middle and ring fingers up and spread. Palm faces forward. |  | Six starts at the little finger. | HK, SSN, LP, BG |
| 7 | Touch your thumb to your ring finger. Hold your index, middle and little fingers up and spread. Palm faces forward. |  | The thumb moves one finger over. | HK, SSN, BG |
| 8 | Touch your thumb to your middle finger. Hold your index, ring and little fingers up and spread. Palm faces forward. |  | The thumb moves one more finger over. | HK, SSN, BG |
| 9 | Touch your thumb to your index finger, making a small loop. Hold your middle, ring and little fingers up and spread. Palm faces forward. |  | The thumb ends at the pointer. It is the same shape as the letter F. | HK, SSN, LP, BG |
| 10 | Make a fist with your thumb sticking up. Palm faces out. | Twist your hand at the wrist back and forth. | Thumbs-up with a little wiggle. | AB, BG |
